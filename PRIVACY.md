# Privacy Policy: Memory Stack

_Last updated: 28 October 2026_

Online version: https://memory-stack-e6990.web.app/privacy.html

Memory Stack helps you revise data-structures and algorithms problems with spaced repetition. This policy explains what the app stores and why.

## What we collect

- **Account details:** your email address and, if you give one, your name. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email from Google.
- **Your study data:** the problems you mark as solved, your notes and code, your review dates, and how many reviews you remembered.

We do not collect your location or contacts, and we do not use advertising or analytics trackers.

## How it is used

Your data is used only to run the app: to sign you in, to show your problems and stats, and to schedule your reviews. It is never sold or shared with third parties for marketing.

## Where it is stored

Data is stored in Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore), and a copy is kept on your device so the app works offline. Firebase security rules allow only your signed-in account to read or write your data. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest by Google.

## Notifications

The app schedules one local reminder a day on your phone. No data is sent for this. You can turn it off in your phone's notification settings.

## Deleting your data

In the app, tap your profile picture (top right) and choose **Delete account**. This permanently deletes your account and all your problems, notes and review history. You can also email us and we will delete it for you within 30 days.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

## Contact

Questions or deletion requests: use the developer email shown on the app's Google Play page.
