Memory Stack: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Memory Stack helps you revise data-structures and algorithms problems with spaced repetition. This policy explains what the app stores and why.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and, if you give one, your name. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email from Google.
  • Your study data: the problems you mark as solved, your notes and code, your aptitude questions, your review dates, and how many reviews you remembered.

We do not collect your location or contacts, and we do not use advertising or analytics trackers.

How it is used

Your data is used only to run the app: to sign you in, to show your problems and stats, and to schedule your reviews. It is never sold or shared with third parties for marketing.

Where it is stored

Data is stored in Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore), and a copy is kept on your device so the app works offline. Security rules allow only your signed-in account to read or write your data. Google encrypts it in transit and at rest.

Running code

When you tap Run, the code and input you typed are sent to a code-execution service (Judge0) so they can be compiled and run, and the output is sent back. Nothing else about you is sent, and we do not keep the code you run unless you save it as your solution.

Leaderboard (optional)

Only if you choose Join the leaderboard, your first name, streak, number of solved problems, level and the title you wear are shown to other signed-in users. Never your email, notes or code. Leaving the leaderboard deletes your entry.

Scanning questions with AI (optional)

If you scan an aptitude question, the photo is sent to Google's Gemini AI (through Firebase AI Logic) to read the question and work out the answer. The photo is not stored by us; only the question, options, answer and explanation you save are kept in your account. On Google's free tier, Google may use what is sent to improve its services, so do not scan anything personal.

LeetCode import (optional)

If you add your LeetCode username in Profile → LeetCode, the app asks LeetCode for your public list of recently accepted problems and adds new ones to your stack. Your username is kept only on your device; it is not stored in our database. Disconnect it any time in the same place.

Notifications

The app schedules one local reminder a day on your phone, at a time you choose in Profile. No data is sent for this. You can turn it off in Profile or in your phone's settings.

Deleting your data

In the app, open Profile and choose Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, problems, solution cards, review totals and any leaderboard entry. You can also email us, and we will delete it within 30 days.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: use the developer email shown on the app's Google Play page.
